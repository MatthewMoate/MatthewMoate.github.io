Keynote speaking

A human story with somewhere practical to go

Matthew brings lived experience into the room with warmth, steadiness and respect. The aim is to create space for a real conversation, then help people see what they can do next.

The keynote frame

Notice.
Show up.
Stay.

Awareness that leads to useful action

The keynote explores the moments when someone senses that a mate, colleague or community member may be struggling, but feels unsure how to respond.

It offers plain language for beginning the conversation and a reminder that support often matters beyond a single check-in.

What the room can take away

A steadier way to respond

  1. 01

    Notice the change

    Recognise the signals and quiet moments that can be easy to miss.

  2. 02

    Begin the conversation

    Use ordinary language, listen and resist the pressure to diagnose or fix.

  3. 03

    Keep checking in

    Understand why connection after the first conversation matters.

Planning the session

Built around the people in the room

The event context, audience and any support pathways available on the day shape the final talk. Sensitive content should sit alongside clear support information and the host organisation's safety process.
