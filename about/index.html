About Matthew

Lived experience, shared with care

Matthew Moate is the person behind Take a Mate. His work focuses on creating space for conversations people often want to have, but do not always know how to begin.

Why Take a Mate exists

Concern can be real even when the right words are hard to find.

Take a Mate grew from Matthew's lived experience and a belief that practical human connection matters. The work helps people recognise the moment, begin the conversation and keep showing up over time.

How Matthew works

Direct, warm and useful

Matthew speaks person to person. He does not present lived experience as a clinical model or a complete answer. He uses it to make the human situation recognisable and to offer a next step people can actually use.

Each engagement starts with the people in the room: what they already know, what may be difficult to say and what appropriate support is available around them.
