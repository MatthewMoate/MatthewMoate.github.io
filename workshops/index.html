Workshops

Make room for an honest conversation

Practical sessions help groups move from good intentions to language and actions they can use in real life.

The approach

People first. Practical. Non-clinical.

Take a Mate workshops create a structured space to reflect on what people notice, what can make it hard to act and how a useful conversation might begin.

The work respects uncertainty. It supports human connection without presenting lived experience as universal expertise or trying to replace clinical care.

A practical session arc

From concern to a next step

01

Name the situation

Use plain, recognisable language for the moments people often hesitate.

02

Explore what helps

Consider realistic ways to start the conversation, listen and stay connected.

03

Set the boundary

Be clear about when and how to connect someone with appropriate support.
