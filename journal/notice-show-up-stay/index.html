Notice. Show up. Stay.

Three practical behaviours for supporting someone when something does not feel quite right.

Take a Mate · 29 September 2026

When someone may be struggling, it can be hard to know what to say. You might worry about getting it wrong, intruding or opening a conversation you cannot fix.

Take a Mate offers a simple way to think about what comes next.

Notice

Pay attention to the change, the signal or the quiet moment that is easy to miss. You do not need to diagnose what is happening. You can begin by recognising that something feels different.

Show up

Create some space and start a genuine conversation. Listen without rushing to solve everything. A useful first step can be as simple as naming what you have noticed and asking how the person is going.

Stay

Keep checking in. Connection matters after the first conversation too. Following up can show that your concern was real and that the person has not been forgotten.

Take a Mate is grounded in lived experience and practical action. It does not replace clinical care. When someone needs professional or urgent support, help them connect with the appropriate service or follow your organisation’s safety process.
